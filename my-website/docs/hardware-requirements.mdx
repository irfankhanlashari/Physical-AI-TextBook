# Hardware Requirements for Physical AI Systems

## Overview

This document outlines the hardware components commonly used in Physical AI applications, including robotics, perception systems, and AI accelerators. The requirements vary significantly based on the specific application, performance needs, and deployment environment.

## Computing Platforms

### Edge AI Computing

#### NVIDIA Jetson Series
| Model | GPU | CPU | Memory | Power | Use Case |
|-------|-----|-----|--------|-------|----------|
| Jetson Nano | 128-core Maxwell | Quad-core ARM A57 | 4GB LPDDR4 | 10W | Entry-level AI |
| Jetson TX2 | 256-core Pascal | Dual Denver 2 + Quad ARM A57 | 8GB LPDDR4 | 15W | Mobile robotics |
| Jetson Xavier NX | 384-core Volta | Hex-core ARM Carmel | 8GB LPDDR4 | 15W | Advanced robotics |
| Jetson AGX Orin | 2048-core Ada Lovelace | 12-core ARM Hercules | 32GB LPDDR5 | 60W | High-performance AI |

#### Other Edge Platforms
- **Intel Movidius Neural Compute Stick**: USB-based AI acceleration
- **Google Coral Edge TPU**: TensorFlow Lite optimized inference
- **Xilinx Zynq SoC**: FPGA-based AI acceleration
- **Qualcomm RB3 Gen 2**: Robotics-focused platform

### Cloud Computing

#### GPU Instances
- **NVIDIA A100**: High-performance training and inference
- **NVIDIA V100**: Established training platform
- **NVIDIA T4**: Efficient inference platform
- **AMD MI250X**: Alternative to NVIDIA GPUs

## Sensors

### Vision Sensors

#### Cameras
- **RGB Cameras**: Standard visual input
  - Resolution: 720p to 4K
  - Frame rate: 30-120 FPS
  - Interface: USB 3.0, GigE, MIPI CSI-2

- **RGB-D Cameras**:
  - Intel RealSense D435: 1280×720 depth resolution
  - Microsoft Kinect Azure: Advanced depth sensing
  - Stereolabs ZED: Stereoscopic vision

#### LiDAR Sensors
| Model | Range | Accuracy | FOV | Price Range | Use Case |
|-------|-------|----------|-----|-------------|----------|
| Velodyne Puck | 100m | 2cm | 360° H × 30° V | $8,000-$12,000 | Outdoor navigation |
| Ouster OS1 | 120m | 3cm | 360° H × 30° V | $8,000-$10,000 | Mapping and navigation |
| Livox Mid-360 | 260m | 3cm | 360° H × 90° V | $1,500-$2,000 | Cost-effective solution |
| Slamtec Tim561 | 25m | 2cm | 270° H × 3.7° V | $3,000-$4,000 | Indoor applications |

### Inertial Sensors

#### IMUs (Inertial Measurement Units)
- **Bosch BNO055**: Integrated sensor with attitude estimation
- **ST LSM6DSOX**: 6-axis IMU with AI processing
- **Analog Devices ADIS16470**: High-performance tactical-grade IMU
- **VectorNav VN-100**: High-precision attitude reference system

#### Gyroscopes and Accelerometers
- **Analog Devices ADXRS453**: High-performance MEMS gyroscope
- **TDK InvenSense ICM-20689**: 6-axis MEMS motion tracking

### Tactile Sensors

#### Force/Torque Sensors
- **ATI Gamma Series**: Multi-axis force/torque sensors
- **Robotiq FT300**: Collaborative robot force/torque sensor
- **Wacoh-Tech SA-100**: High-precision force sensor

#### Tactile Skin
- **BioTAC**: Biomimetic tactile sensor
- **GelSight**: Vision-based tactile sensing
- **Digit**: Low-cost tactile sensor

## Communication and Networking

### Wireless Communication
- **Wi-Fi 6/6E**: High-bandwidth communication

- **Bluetooth 5.x**: Short-range, low-power communication

### Wired Communication
- **Ethernet**: High-speed, reliable communication
- **CAN Bus**: Robust automotive/industrial communication
- **RS-485**: Long-distance serial communication
- **USB 3.0/3.1**: High-speed peripheral communication

## Simulation Hardware

### High-Performance Computing for Simulation

## Power Systems

### Batteries
- **LiPo (Lithium Polymer)**: High power density, common in mobile robots
- **LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate)**: Safer, longer cycle life
- **SLA (Sealed Lead Acid)**: Lower cost, heavier

### Power Management
- **DC-DC Converters**: Voltage regulation
- **Power Distribution Boards**: Efficient power routing
- **Battery Management Systems**: Safe charging/discharging

## Cost Considerations

### Budget Categories

#### Low Budget ($1,000 - $5,000)
- Raspberry Pi-based systems
- Basic sensors and actuators
- Simple mobile platforms
- Educational robot kits

#### Mid-Range ($5,000 - $25,000)
- NVIDIA Jetson platforms
- Quality sensors (LiDAR, cameras)
- Collaborative robot arms
- Professional mobile bases

#### High-End ($25,000+)
- Advanced humanoid robots
- High-performance computing
- Precision sensors and actuators
- Specialized platforms

### Cost Optimization Strategies
- Use simulation for initial development
- Leverage open-source software
- Consider refurbished or academic pricing
- Plan for scalability and reuse

## Key Concepts Summary
